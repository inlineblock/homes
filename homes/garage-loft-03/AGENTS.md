# Garage Loft 03

- User brief: a two-bay garage that stores four passenger/sports cars using a KLAUS-style lift with a pit below grade; a heated/cooled game room and pool table occupy the floor above.
- Use KLAUS MultiBase 2072i double-platform geometry as a planning reference. The reusable lift is an original schematic proxy, not manufacturer CAD, an approved installation, or an endorsed reproduction.
- Show two cars at driveway level and two below in the stored state. Show the alternate raised state separately; the upper cars rise above grade when lower cars are retrieved. Preserve the full swept clearance zone.
- Read `references/README.md` for current manufacturer sources, exact reference limits, and unresolved fit. Do not substitute the earlier pit-free SingleVario assumption.
- Keep the upper room thermally/acoustically separated from the garage. Model dedicated upstairs heat-pump equipment; do not imply a calculated HVAC capacity or share garage return air.
- Keep clear pool cue space, a separate pedestrian stair entrance, stairwell openings, and access landings in every plan and model revision.
- Output layout and history policy come from `homes/AGENTS.md`. Inspect the exterior, game room, and pit/lift section before promoting output files.

## Human workflow is a required quality gate
- Before rendering, trace parking, driver exit, walking off the stationary deck, operating from fixed floor, entering the stair lobby, climbing, and leaving through the pedestrian entrance. A control box or staircase alone does not resolve those workflows.
- Preserve the fixed front apron, independent operator bay, actual internal doorway, clear leaf swing, and ground/upper landings. Do not route circulation across the pit or assume a parked vehicle or moving deck is a permanent walkway.
- Check the fixed-floor pedestrian route with both lift positions and doors open; inspect it in a plan and a ground-floor cutaway. Record what remains unverified, especially actual car-door swing and operator sightlines around cars.
- Show the access-protection reservation. Schematic guards, paint, and geometry tests must never be described as a certified lift safety system; coordinate the opening mechanism and interlocks with the supplier.

## Visual fidelity
- The user rejected the blocky placeholder look. Presentation views should use curved vehicle bodies, believable glazing and wheels, restrained physical-scale materials, detailed furniture, and composed architectural lighting/landscaping. More pixels alone do not meet that request.
- Keep presentation quality in the editable Blender model. Do not substitute an unrelated generated beauty image for evidence of the model's layout.
- Keep technical overlays and cutaways legible, and ensure visual detail does not obscure unresolved design conditions. Inspect preview renders before spending time on final sampling.
- Keep the home README and output index complete: at least three full-envelope exterior angles, two room/interior views, both occupied-level floor plans, and dedicated lift-feature imagery. Technical pit/circulation cutaways do not count as exterior coverage. Preserve explicit stored/raised labels and unresolved supplier safety notes.
