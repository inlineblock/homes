## Placement and operation

- Face width: 762 mm; cabinet body width: 723.9 mm; body depth: 596.9 mm. Overall closed depth, including pulls: 715.9 mm.
- Bottom-center origin, front −Y, Z up. Body support platform at local Z +6 mm. Opening width 730 mm; see manifest for the height and depth of this option.
- Two real bottom-hinge assemblies rotate +90° around local X. The measured swept projection is 571.4 mm ahead of the cabinet face; reserve 600 mm more for an operator as a concept allowance.
- The lower handle extends 39 mm below the component origin when open. Reserve at least 45 mm of below-origin clearance in front. This is intended for raised cabinet installation.
- Native previews use CPU Cycles. The gray support pedestal belongs to the preview studio, not the appliance.
