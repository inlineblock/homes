# Templates

- Templates must be neutral starting points; do not carry Atrium 01 facts into another home.
- Keep required manifests small, document optional fields, and ensure examples parse.
- Match the structure documented in `homes/AGENTS.md`. Add empty tracked folders only where they guide actual work.
- Scaffold `outputs/images/`, `outputs/plans/`, and `outputs/README.md` for every new home. Follow the current-output and visual-index rules in `homes/AGENTS.md`; do not scaffold output history folders.

- New homes include a filled `program.md` using the canonical checklist; record required/optional/excluded features and actual evidence status.
- Templates should route to the handbook and project skills rather than duplicate their full instructions.
