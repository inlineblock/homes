# Modern Block — visual reference notes

The following observations come from inspected exterior and interior photographs. Source identities, addresses, identifying links and downloaded photographs are intentionally excluded from this repository. Photographs establish visible appearance and a few spatial relationships; they do not establish measured dimensions or a complete floor plan.

## Observed architectural character

- Low pale limestone pavilions frame an open planted courtyard. A taller rectangular upper volume has vertical timber cladding, large dark-framed windows and thin horizontal roof bands.
- The arrival combines a broad informal stone path, planted edges and generous stone steps. The dark entry door is recessed beside full-height dining-room glazing.
- A deep covered outdoor room opens through tall pivoting horizontal timber louver panels. It contains seating and an outdoor preparation counter and connects separate enclosed volumes.
- Large sliding glazing connects the kitchen and living spaces to a courtyard with lawn, a pool and a mature canopy tree. Roof edges are restrained and nearly level in the photographs; actual roof falls and drainage are unverified.
- The courtyard-facing upper level includes a deep covered balcony with a transparent guard, suspended lounge chairs and slender perimeter posts. A covered dining terrace sits beneath it. The upper roof is a canopy over this occupied balcony, not an occupied rooftop.
- The kitchen has a long pale veined-stone waterfall island parallel to the courtyard glazing, warm timber cabinetry, a broad range with a dark hood and brass-colored hardware. Narrow timber ceiling battens surround a rectangular skylight. A small dark shelved study is visible beyond one end; the lounge is beyond the other.
- The living room combines full-height courtyard glass with a pale stone fireplace wall, a low linear firebox, soft neutral seating and a warm sculptural chair.
- The upper stair gallery has a long skylight, pale vertical timber wall panels, timber stair treads and transparent guards. Photographs do not reveal the full stair geometry.
- The bathing area has a freestanding tub before full-height glass to a private planted stone court, a separate glass shower and a floating double vanity with two integrated stone basins and wall-mounted taps. The court contains an outdoor shower. Exact bathroom dimensions and the toilet enclosure are not established.
- A bedroom opens toward the pool through full-height glass. Additional images show children's bedrooms and bathrooms, timber display/counter storage beside the stair, a dark teal bar, and a separate fireplace lounge. Their complete floor-plan connections are not visible.

## Interpretation and missing evidence

No measured architectural floor plan was found in the inspected public gallery material. Modern Block therefore uses a coordinated original concept layout to connect the observed architectural elements. The user authorized creative interpretation of missing rooms, views and spatial information.

Room sizes, complete bedroom/bathroom count, service spaces, garage arrangement, circulation, roof build-up, drainage routes and concealed systems are design assumptions unless independently recorded as model dimensions. The project's own area schedule describes only its authored geometry. It must not be presented as the measured area of the photographed building.

The landscape is a conceptual setting. Property boundaries, orientation, grades, setbacks, existing tree locations, soil, utilities and climatic performance have not been surveyed or engineered. Current render views should remain mutually consistent with the editable native model, and synthesized photographic studies must be labeled as interpretations of those native views.

## Reference handling

Third-party photographs were inspected only as research. They are not copied into project outputs, textures, assets or the shared library. Shared assets and authored model geometry have their own provenance records. This project is an editable visualization and dimensioned architectural concept, not an as-built, permit set or construction document.
