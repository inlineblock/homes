# Shared dressing/storage interface

- Preserve the exact published native and declared collection; keep scale 1. Use a separately published sibling for another size.
- Read asset.json for origin, front direction and equipment/operation allowances before placement. Do not overlay complete carcasses or strip doors from a linked asset in the home.
- The published model is in its stated closed/open fixed state. Pose a separate copy for operation review; preserve all actual shelves, rods, baskets and drawers.
- Sample garments, books and shoes show illustrative use, not a guaranteed household capacity. Upper storage needs an explicit safe reach strategy.
- Isolated asset verification does not establish host circulation, fastening, load capacity or chosen product approval.
