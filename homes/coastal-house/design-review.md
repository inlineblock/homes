# Coastal House design review

## Reviewed revision

Front arrival, low pitched roofs, parking, guest storage and a more generous entry were revised together in the native model and drawings. This review distinguishes measured concept geometry from unresolved site and construction decisions. It does not certify compliance with the repository's full standard or local regulations.
