# Counter-depth integrated refrigeration allowance

The A proposal replaces the prior deep generic body with two 24-inch integrated columns, using the Sub-Zero DEC2450R refrigerator and DEC2450FI freezer as dimensional references. Both official quick-reference guides state 24 W × 84 H × 24 D inches, with a 25-inch-deep installation opening. The plan reserves that full 25-inch enclosure depth for both the appliance and upper joinery. Adjoining cabinet fronts align without deepening the entire counter run.

The south end remains 10.5 inches clear of the pantry return face, and a 9-inch north filler separates the appliance from the next cabinet. These are planning allowances; selected hinges, handles, dual installation kit, panel thickness, ventilation and drawer removal still require coordinated checking.
