# Design basis and opening references

The house, furniture, materials and coastal setting are original procedural concept geometry. The coast is illustrative: no actual site, survey, orientation or flood elevation was supplied. No third-party images, CAD or textures are redistributed.

The requested mechanism is a multi-slide pocket system, with a separate counter-height pass-through. Primary manufacturer references reviewed September 17, 2026:

- [Fleetwood Series 3070-T pocket-door details](https://www.fleetwoodusa.net/Documents_Guide/Products/3070-T/Arch_Dwgs/3070-T-03__Pocket_Door1.pdf): pocket jamb, sill-pan and removable/access considerations. This establishes the mechanism, not approval of our dimensions or simplified profiles.
- [Andersen pass-through windows](https://www.andersenwindows.com/windows-and-doors/windows/pass-through-windows): separate-track sliding panels can stack or pocket into a wall; the page illustrates a continuous indoor/outdoor countertop and a flush-sill concept.

The six-panel 30-foot door and four-panel 12-foot window are original spatial placeholders, not manufacturer CAD. Panel weights, exact tracks, structural header, weather resistance, drainage, thermal separation, pocket access, wind/impact requirements and insect screens require product and site coordination. The nearly continuous counter has a narrow recessed track zone; it is not a certified waterproof or thermal detail.
