# Inset kitchen kit

Cream inset cabinetry, oak island modules, concealed equipment and aged-brass details form a reusable kitchen family. These are original concept assets by Homes project contributors, licensed CC BY 4.0. They are not commercial products or installation specifications.

All entries below are **v001**. Images are actual native asset previews. Sizes are measured closed bounds, **width × depth × height**, including hardware projections; inches are rounded, with millimeters in parentheses. Nominal module names may differ from their full housing size. Material swatches have no fixed object dimensions.

The kit contains **27 assets: 12 cabinet modules, five appliances, seven hardware/fixture assemblies and three finishes**. Their linked receipts record successful native reopening and completed isolated visual review. The double-shade pendant uses the final 1.35 m suspension drop.

**Adoption and evidence:** The Modern Block selections below are linked in its native model and have passed checked concept installation, as recorded in the [host verification receipt](../homes/modern-block/model/kitchen-validation.json) and [home component review](../homes/modern-block/kitchen-components.md). The three unused alternatives remain library options. Individual asset receipts establish isolated reopening and visual review; the separate host receipt checks installed geometry, explicit open states and reserved operator space. These concept checks do not establish product-specific installation, accessibility or engineering approval.
