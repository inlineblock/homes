## Design and program

The selected scheme follows the recorded pre-model [design intent](design-intent.md): limestone pavilions surround a pool court, a cedar upper bar holds the family bedrooms, and a covered operable-screen lanai connects the guest/office wing. The [program](program.md) and [independent plan review](design-review-notes.md) document four bedrooms, three full baths plus powder, two-car parking, complete kitchen equipment, clothing and household storage, laundry and mechanical reservations.

Conditioned gross floor area is 527.96 m² / approximately 5,683 sq ft: ground main 240 m², guest wing 96 m², upper 191.96 m² after the 12.04 m² stair void. Garage 64 m² / approximately 689 sq ft is additional. Gross enclosed area is 591.96 m² / 6,371.80 sq ft. Walls are included; lanai, balcony, pool, open terraces and roofs are excluded.

The 22 stair risers are 163.64 mm, treads 280 mm, flight width 1.2 m, half landing 1.22 m and upper landing 1.2 m. The upper tread meets the 3.6 m datum. The full-height stair opening is retained; detailed headroom, guard, structure and code review remain professional work. Five pivot screens have 2.0 m centers and 0.903 m swept radius. Their open and closed states use the identical camera. Covered doors at both pavilion ends connect directly to the lanai.

## Remaining concept limitations

No survey, parcel, orientation, actual grade, soil or tree/root information was supplied. The landscape and drainage outlet are illustrative. Structural capacity, foundation engineering, waterproofing, roof drainage capacity/overflow, glazing/frame design, pool barrier and hydraulics, fire/life safety, accessibility and approvals remain unverified. Window modules have explicit concept resizing and therefore variable frame sightlines. Privacy/shading, selected hardware, appliances and sanitary products, installation envelopes, MEP sizing and control layouts require specification. The ground guest route is sheltered but not an accessibility certification. Generic physical reservations are not installed systems.

These limits describe the concept stage; they do not excuse open modeled collisions or missing gallery coverage. The user authorized committing and pushing the completed anonymous concept and subsequent revision.
