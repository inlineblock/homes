# Native model and architectural export

- Preserve the front composition and roof silhouette from the user's reference. The interior is a schematic proposal and can change.
- Keep the five versioned library links relative. Cedar and metal materials are shared assets; actual board joints and standing seams are modeled geometry.
- `timber-courtyard-02.blend` is the furnished visualization; its IFC is a classified architectural geometry export. They do not automatically synchronize.
- Keep roof and structure in separately switchable collections. Do not destroy the full exterior to obtain a plan or cutaway.
- After changes, measure slab geometry, check three modeled beds/bathrooms, reopen the saved file, and validate the IFC before calling the deliverables ready.
