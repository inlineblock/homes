# Modern Block — independent visual review

Status: **all twelve native views accepted for concept presentation**. No outstanding visual blocker remains in the reviewed native gallery. This review does not establish construction readiness. Source identities and third-party imagery are excluded from this record.

## Review coverage

Actual pixels were inspected in all twelve native views at 1800 × 1200 and both furnished concept plan previews. Review progressed through draft and corrected renders. Accepted cameras: front arrival, courtyard pool, elevated roof/site, open lanai, kitchen, living, primary bedroom, primary vanity, closed lanai, pool/balcony, stair gallery and tub/shower. The two lanai states use the same unobstructed camera. Photographic studies are outside this native-gallery review.

## Corrections verified

- Front foundation closes the previously visible gap to the concept grade; broad entrance steps remain visible.
- The revised elevated camera shows the pool, pavilion relationships, covered balcony, rooflights and garage access without exposing the edge of the illustrative ground plane.
- Both lanai views now show the screen leaves clearly; the earlier tree-trunk obstruction is gone.
- The pool/balcony camera is outside the garage wall and produces a clear image of its subject.
- Kitchen refrigeration, island, sink and rear cooking wall are visible together. The ceiling battens and actual rooflight aperture are legible.
- The living camera shows the seating faces, coffee table and fireplace; the clipped stair fragment is absent.
- Pale bathing-room finishes and corrected illumination make the room readable. Separate views demonstrate the double vanity and the tub with separate shower screen and tray. The vanity was moved away from the dressing-door approach.
- Planting is grouped more densely, with small planted areas in the court. The restrained landscape remains a concept setting.
- The upper plan now labels the stair void separately from the adjacent linen/landing zone.

## Final stair correction verified

The revised stair camera and exposure reveal the stair opening, treads and transparent guards. Review identified one recessed light mistakenly placed in the stair void, appearing on a tread. The author removed it from the canonical scene and corrected the placement rules. Pixel inspection of the final stair render confirms that the misplaced fixture is gone. The regenerated pool/balcony view was also inspected and remains clear.

## Accepted presentation limits

These are editable native concept renders with restrained materials and simplified context. Stone texture is subtle; foreground cabinetry occupies the right edge of the kitchen view; the left vanity is partly cropped in its detail view, complemented by the plan and full bathing view. These do not obscure the principal architectural subjects. Photographic finishing may enrich appearance but must remain labeled and cannot substitute for the native geometry evidence.

The review does not certify structure, stair/guard regulations, pool barriers, waterproofing, exact product installation, energy performance, surveyed site conditions or permit compliance. Plans use project-authored dimensions and an explicit area convention.
