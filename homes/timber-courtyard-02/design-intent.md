# Timber Courtyard — kitchen revision intent

## Why the revision exists

The actual walkthrough exposed an unfinished kitchen, not a rendering problem. The old island left approximately 29.4 inches to the rear counter; the sink and refrigerator were proxies, an oven and extraction hood were absent, and tall tile stood in front of glazing. The user specifically asked for real cooking equipment, storage, an oven and a pantry. Independent layout and asset reviews informed this revision.

## Directions considered

1. Refine the old rear-wall arrangement: rejected because cooking/extraction would remain awkward against the garden glazing.
2. Garden-facing cleanup counter, east cooking/service wall and transverse island: selected. It separates cleanup, cooking, storage and seating while preserving the courtyard-led exterior.
3. Clear rear glass with an island sink: rejected because sink and dishwasher would consume the useful preparation and social surface.

## Selected design

- Retain the paired timber wings, roofs, courtyard, front composition and 2,400 sq ft gross enclosed footprint. Revise the kitchen and its pantry; do not imply the remaining legacy program is resolved.
- Move a 36-inch induction cooktop, separate 30-inch oven and 36-inch hood to the east pantry wall. Provide an actual oven housing, countertop cutout, landing areas and a continuous conceptual exhaust route.
- Add a 48-inch panel-ready refrigerator/freezer, detailed smoked-oak cabinet modules, waste/recycling pullout and integrated dishwasher. Keep cleanup under the rear garden window.
- Turn the island across the room: approximately 7 ft 9 in by 4 ft, three seating places, storage on its kitchen side and a supported knee recess. Target approximately four-foot work aisles, measured to actual projecting fronts and handles.
- Move the pantry doorway south to y=33.5–36.5 ft. Fit the existing 8 × 16 ft pantry/storage room with food shelves, appliance storage, worktop and lighting. Opening/service space is part of the design.
- Replace the tall tile screen in front of glass with a low stone splash and a properly enclosed cedar-clad spandrel under the kitchen window. Preserve rear glazing above counter height and the living/dining full-height glazing. Record this limited rear elevation change rather than pretending the windows are unchanged.
- Keep cedar structure; use restrained smoked oak, pale honed stone, bronze details and sage tile concentrated behind the cooking wall. Add a finished timber ceiling lining, deliberate task/ambient lighting and supported island lighting. Replace the adjacent schematic chairs, coffee table and sofa with supported shared furniture. Raise the pantry side window above its seven-foot shelving and provide solid anti-tip backing.

## Asset and verification priorities

Use the exact compatible shared appliance, fixture, material and furniture versions; contribute missing smoked-oak cabinet and integrated-appliance variants as immutable library assets, then explicitly adopt them. Exact-fit worktops, enclosure, ceiling and duct route remain project-specific.

Before promotion: inspect normal-eye-height kitchen/cooking/pantry views, furnished plan, rear exterior and courtyard views; measure appliance openings, door/drawer/seat clearances, countertop cutouts, hood and ceiling clearances, lighting mounting and relative asset links. Refresh the native/IFC/plan/gallery together. Refresh affected photographic studies and the walkthrough only after the underlying kitchen passes review.
