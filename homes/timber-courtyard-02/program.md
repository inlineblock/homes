# Timber Courtyard program

Status: kitchen revision in progress; legacy whole-home program remains unresolved.

The confirmed brief is an exterior-led, single-level, three-bedroom/three-bath courtyard home, approximately 2,400 sq ft gross enclosed including walls. The 62 × 48 ft footprint excludes the 24 × 24 ft open courtyard. Site/climate/road and parking are illustrative or unresolved. The current revision adopts the user's premium kitchen preferences; it does not claim the old bathrooms, bedrooms or services meet the later standard.
