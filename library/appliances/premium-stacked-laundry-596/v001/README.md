## Manufacturer dimensional precedent

The current [Miele WXR960 WCS washer](https://www.mieleusa.com/product/12864100/w1-front-loading-washing-machine-wxr960-wcs-tdosandintensewash) and [TXR960WP Eco&Steam dryer](https://www.mieleusa.com/product/12864160/t1-heat-pump-dryer-txr960wp-ecoandsteam) provide the individual 596 × 643 × 850 mm size and 1077 mm door-open depth. The dryer page lists the matching washer and WTV502/WTV512 kits. Dealer stock is unverified.

The **1711 mm** WTV502 stack height comes from Miele’s [preceding W1/T1 family diagram](https://www.mieleusa.com/forms2/us/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=10669410). The current pages/manuals confirm the same kit family and individual heights; the exact current installed-height drawing must still be confirmed. This is why the host reserves 1800 mm rather than treating the native geometry height as an approved installation dimension.

## Services and installation limits

The reference units are 120 V, 60 Hz, 15 A with NEMA 5-15 connections. Coordinate circuits or the approved pair adapter with selected instructions. Provide accessible washer shutoffs and separate trapped drainage; a stain sink is not automatically the appliance drain system. The ventless dryer still needs heat dissipation, accessible front intake/filter and condensate management. Do not enclose it behind sealed decorative doors or bury its service access.

See the official [washer manual, installation pages 76–81](https://media.miele.com/downloads/c9/f5/00_C7F1383867AF1FD1898B2B80E678C9F5.pdf) and [dryer manual, installation pages 74–76 and technical data 92](https://media.miele.com/downloads/03/33/00_C7F13838CDF91FD186920BC33FF60333.pdf). Selected kit, floor capacity, vibration, leak management, hoses, ventilation and jurisdiction-specific requirements remain project work.
