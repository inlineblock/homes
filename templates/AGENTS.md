# Templates

- Templates must be neutral starting points; do not carry Atrium 01 facts into another home.
- Keep required manifests small, document optional fields, and ensure examples parse.
- Match the structure documented in the root README. Add empty tracked folders only where they guide actual work.
- Scaffold `outputs/images/`, `outputs/plans/`, and `outputs/README.md` for every new home. Follow the current-output and visual-index rules in `homes/AGENTS.md`; do not scaffold output history folders.
