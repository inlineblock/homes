# Twin Gables Courtyard — current outputs

![Private timber arrival beneath the twin gables](images/photo-hero.png)

*AI photographic study of the reviewed native design; not a built-home photograph.*

Two primary suites, each with its own walk-in closet, double vanity, separate tub and shower, enclosed toilet room and linen closet. A separate office/guest room, complete kitchen, fitted pantry and planted courtyard support everyday living.

**3,360 sq ft gross planning area · two dedicated bedrooms + office/guest · three baths · one occupied level.** Includes wall allowances; excludes the 720 sq ft courtyard, parking, terraces and roof overhangs.

The private street facade gives way to tall vaulted living spaces and a glazed rear garden elevation. Steel framing is a concept assumption; visible beams use nonstructural wood-look covers. Clear ceiling heights, equipment and door operation are recorded in the design review.
