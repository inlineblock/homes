# Timber Courtyard walkthrough

[Play the Blender geometry base](timber-courtyard-native.mp4) · [Editable camera scene](../../model/timber-walkthrough.blend) · [Home](../../README.md)

A continuous 30-second camera route: approach → glazed entry → planted courtyard → living room → kitchen. This is actual movement through the Blender scene at a fixed 25 mm lens and 5 ft 6 in eye height, with real perspective and parallax. There are no slideshow transitions or digital zooms. The H.264 video is 1280 × 720 at 24 fps, with 721 frames and no soundtrack.

The native clip is a **geometry base**, showing the revised kitchen with a modeled induction cooktop, built-in oven, extraction hood, wide integrated refrigerator, sink, dishwasher, working drawer storage and a fitted pantry. The connected living area uses shared supported furniture. Three sliding leaves are opened in this separate presentation scene; the canonical home model is unchanged. These opening states are not an engineered glazing specification, and the remaining legacy bedrooms, bathrooms and whole-home program retain their separately recorded limitations.

**Current AI-finishing status:** the revised native animation and all fifteen paired reference checkpoints are reviewed. A refreshed motion-conditioned AI film is being generated and will be published only after review. Earlier AI candidates and derived boundaries belong to the obsolete kitchen and are not current deliverables.

## Verification and reproduction

[Camera route and presentation overrides](camera-route.json) · [Fresh-process native verification](native-verification.json)

The current native MP4 decodes fully. Eight high-sample keyframes, fifteen reference checkpoints and sixty-one half-second sequence samples were inspected. A conservative 700 mm wide, 1.80 m high body sweep across all 721 camera poses and 720 connecting segments clears 2,063 evaluated fixed meshes. The closest additional bounds gap is about 142 mm beside a dining-chair back. Vegetation/landscape objects and floor contact below 50 mm are excluded; this is a concept camera-route check, not accessibility or construction approval. GUI playback of this revision was unavailable because the Mac was locked.

The separately reopened Blender scene verifies all 721 camera poses, constant lens and eye height, three opened leaves and the current pinned relative shared-library dependencies. The canonical source hash is unchanged. These checks do not constitute a new whole-home design approval. See [native video verification](video-verification.json), [AI review](ai-review.json) and [runtime provenance](ai-runtime.json) for exact evidence and limits.

From the repository root, with Blender 4.5.14 LTS and FFmpeg available:

```sh
blender --factory-startup --background --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/timber02/walkthrough.py -- --build --keyframes
blender --factory-startup --background --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/timber02/walkthrough.py -- --animate
ffmpeg -framerate 24 -start_number 1 -i homes/timber-courtyard-02/outputs/work/walkthrough/frames/frame-%04d.png -frames:v 721 -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart homes/timber-courtyard-02/outputs/work/walkthrough/review-native.mp4
```

Review the complete candidate before replacing the current MP4 or running `walkthrough.py -- --promote --review-note "Describe the actual completed review"`. Then run `verify_walkthrough.py` in a fresh Blender process. Keep frame sequences and experimental AI clips in ignored `outputs/work/`; overwrite current deliverables after review instead of keeping revision folders.

The AI test uses [ltx-2-mlx](https://github.com/dgrauet/ltx-2-mlx) with LTX-2.3 Union Control, edge frames from the actual Blender animation and Image Gen reference conditioning. It runs locally. Model weights and the isolated runtime are outside Git. Render a four-second test, visually compare it with the native motion, then continue the full route:

```sh
python3 tools/timber02/finish_walkthrough.py --runtime /path/to/ltx-2-mlx --models /path/to/models --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg
python3 tools/timber02/finish_walkthrough.py --runtime /path/to/ltx-2-mlx --models /path/to/models --ffmpeg /path/to/ffmpeg --continue-tour
```

The finisher records fingerprints of actual native frames, references, prompts, runtime settings and output bytes. Changed inputs invalidate cached clips, and each following clip receives the preceding clip's actual endpoint. The native renderer separately binds its frame cache to the canonical model, presentation scene, authoring settings and linked assets. Rebuild and review after a design change; file existence alone never establishes a current or reviewed film. AI candidates remain in ignored work until their motion, geometry and every segment join have been reviewed.
