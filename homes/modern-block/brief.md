# Modern Block

Create an editable architectural concept named Modern Block from user-supplied visual direction. The user authorizes creative infill for missing layout, dimensions and unseen angles.

Retain a warm limestone pavilion base, cedar upper block, slender dark glazing, central pool court, mature tree-led landscape and operable timber-screen lanai. Provide a complete premium household program, coordinated storage, kitchen equipment, bathing, stairs and two-car parking.

Deliver reproducible Blender sources, classified concept IFC, dimensioned furnished concept plans, reviewed native views and clearly labeled AI photographic studies derived from them. This is an independent dimensional/layout interpretation, not a measured existing-building model or construction documentation.

Repository privacy convention for this project: do not include identifying reference-project names, people, location, address, social-media links or source-property URLs in code, documentation, artifacts, metadata or commits. Keep source research outside the repository. No third-party reference images are redistributed. No external publication or contact is authorized.
