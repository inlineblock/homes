# Atrium 01

- User brief: modern Eichler-inspired, around 2,400 sq ft, three bedrooms, three bathrooms. The kitchen is the design priority.
- Maintain the low roofline, expressed post-and-beam character, indoor/outdoor glazing, and open-air atrium. This is an original interpretation, not a reproduction of a historic house plan.
- Area basis: 60 × 44 ft outer footprint minus a 16 × 15 ft open atrium = 2,400 sq ft gross enclosed area including wall thickness. No garage is included. Do not call it net usable or appraised living area.
- Kitchen palette: warm walnut, pale stone, sage fluted ceramic, restrained dark metal, warm pendants. Preserve the island, work surfaces, and practical access when revising the look.
- Keep `tools/atrium01/` inputs, the architectural IFC, the presentation Blender scene, and concept plan consistent. These are separate deliverables; changes do not synchronize automatically.
- Evaluate the kitchen at eye level and the whole layout from above. Inspect saved renders for intersections, missing surfaces, blocked openings, and lighting defects.
- The revised ring has a sheltered carport approach and an enclosed cross-gallery: no bedroom or shared room requires crossing the open court. Preserve actual door, cabinet and appliance operation when refining it.
- Coordinate the selected 10 ft 6 in ceiling plane, 9 ft 6 in beam clearance and 9 ft service ceilings; do not inherit a generic wall height. Bedroom shades have raised/lowered library states and are privacy concepts, not tested blackout products.
