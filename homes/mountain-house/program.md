# Mountain House program and decisions

Profile: **premium**, reflecting the user's modern wood-clad mountain brief and subsequent kitchen/bath/hardware/lighting preferences. Two levels total is confirmed. Three bedrooms and 2.5 baths, a 24 x 24 ft garage and 3,528 sq ft conditioned gross are concept assumptions. Site, orientation, climate loads and jurisdiction are illustrative/unverified.

| Feature | Selection and reason | Modeled provision / evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival/parking | Required; integrated two-car garage at front road | 20 ft garage door, 27 ft drive court, separate entry path, 8 ft foyer and garage-to-mudroom route. Vehicle turning/survey not certified. |
| Coat/drop/storage | Required | Linked mudroom wardrobe bays and bench; each bedroom has usable clothes storage; lower laundry has linen bays. Exact household storage capacity needs a household inventory. |
| Primary suite | Premium user defaults | 14 x 14 ft bedroom zone, 6 x 10 ft dressing zone, 20 x 12 ft bathroom zone with separate 5 ft shower, shared 6 ft tub, double vanity and enclosed 5 x 6 ft WC. Nominal wall-centerline zones, not net room dimensions. |
| Kitchen equipment | Required complete equipment set | Shared 36 in induction cooktop, 30 in oven, hood with exterior duct allowance, dishwasher, undermount sink/mixer, pantry, waste/recycling and 48 in cabinet-matched refrigerator. Generic concept assets, no manufacturer selected. |
| Kitchen operation | Working island, no island seating crowding the aisle | Rear sink/prep and dishwasher; island cooking/oven; separate pantry and fridge. Product door/drawer/service requirements remain selection checks. |
| Living/fireplace | Fireplace selected for Mountain | Shared closed-glass insert with noncombustible chase, hearth and flue allowance. Fuel/appliance approval, combustion air, clearances, chimney and hood-pressure interaction remain unresolved engineering/product choices. |
| Other bedrooms | Required storage/daylight/privacy | Lower bedrooms face exposed side/rear forest elevations, each with 6 ft of linked wardrobe bays. Window/egress product and local rules not verified. |
| Laundry/service | Required | Washer/dryer, folding surface, linen; dedicated mechanical and workshop allowance. System sizing, access/replacement equipment dimensions and lower wastewater connection are not engineered. |
| Lighting | Recessed/task base, one deliberate dining focal fixture | Shared downlights in actual ceiling apertures, kitchen task bar and linear dining pendant. Illustrative lighting power, no rated photometric/energy design. |
| Hardware | Coordinated matte black | Linked bar pulls and paired interior door levers; appliance/storage modules retain their own documented hardware. Recessed-pull alternatives remain catalog options. |
| Deck/outdoor | Required generous view-facing outdoor room | 14 ft rear deck, 8 ft side return, pergola, guards/support concept and lower patio grading bench. Spaced boards provide shade, not a demonstrated dry roof; a drained under-deck/waterproofing assembly remains a design requirement if dry shelter is desired. |
| Envelope/roof | Predominantly wood, low mono-pitch | Timber cladding/soffits, standing-seam appearance, front gutter/downpipes. The 0.66:12 roof needs an appropriate tested system and snow/ice retention/drainage design; no generic standing-seam approval implied. |
| Excluded for this concept | Deliberate rather than forgotten | Pool, sauna, elevator, scullery, wine room and additional garage/lift. Accessibility adaptations and alternate fireplace types remain possible future brief choices. |
