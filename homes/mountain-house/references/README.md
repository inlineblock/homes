# Design references and assumptions

User brief: modern mountain home, two levels total (confirmed main + walkout), built-in garage at front road, land descending to rear, dense woods, predominantly wood exterior, generous rear deck and balcony/patio space.

Original design interpretation: low timber-clad profile at road; 11 ft floor-to-floor difference exposes the rear lower level; 14 ft rear deck and 8 ft side return; partial pergola with a protected lower patio. Three bedrooms and 2.5 baths are concept assumptions, not additional user-specified requirements.

All illustrated forest, terrain and building geometry is original. No actual site, manufacturer roof system, engineered member sizes or permitting conditions are represented as verified.
