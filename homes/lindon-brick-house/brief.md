# Lindon Brick House — design brief

## Direction

Reinterpret the house at **1044 E Center Street, Lindon, Utah 84042** as a modern red-brick home that feels substantial and familiar, with clean contemporary details and better windows. The user's latest direction—“Modern red brick that feels old school and modern. So maybe not farmhouse style”—replaces the earlier modern-farmhouse idea.

Use warm, textured red brick with believable module size, mortar joints, subtle color variation and properly expressed opening reveals. Proposed windows should be larger where useful, rectangular and coordinated, with slender dark frames. Review daylight, room use, privacy and proportions together; a blanket replacement of every wall with glass would not satisfy the brief. Roof edges, trim, entrances and garage doors should support the quieter contemporary composition. Roof covering and technical assemblies remain to be selected.

The latest direction is a brick base with quieter upper volumes in charcoal and subdued mushroom/taupe. The entire stacked-window bay immediately right of the entry should be charcoal, including both storeys, side returns and its gable. The user rejected the cream revision as stark white and its full-height light strips as too contrasty. Keep brick across the lower connectors; use the shared mushroom mineral plaster upstairs, wrapping complete forms rather than alternating narrow stripes. The near-black roof and limited smoked-oak entrance remain. Retain recognizable gables with fewer competing projections; do not copy the supplied style reference's unrelated floor plan or arch.

The low front study wing currently has an unoccupied roof, not an accessible rooftop patio. Slim its edge and retain rearward fall. It has no terrace door, deck finish or usable guard. A future occupied terrace would need deliberate upstairs access and coordinated plan, waterproofing, drainage, guard and structural design; the two existing rear terraces remain the outdoor living spaces in this revision.

## Preserve the recognizable house

Keep the angled attached three-car garage wing and front arrival, the main and upper stories over a walkout basement, two rear decks, and the pool/court relationship to the house and yard. These relationships distinguish this project from a generic new-build concept. Larger openings and simplified details are proposed alterations; their structural and enclosure consequences remain unresolved.

The baseline is seven bedrooms, with five upstairs and two in the basement. The listing identifies two full baths, two three-quarter baths and one half bath: **five physical bathrooms**, not a silently converted decimal count. Preserve the main and basement kitchen and laundry provisions on the upper and basement levels unless a proposed change is explicitly recorded.

## Source and dimensional basis

The current MLS states 2,448 sq ft on the main level, 2,180 upstairs and 2,179 in the basement, totaling 6,807 sq ft. These are listing estimates associated with an appraisal sketch, not independently measured dimensions or a guaranteed model area. See [reference evidence and discrepancies](references/README.md).

Three actual source floor plans were located and visually checked. Their filenames say **Without Dim**. The tour's “2nd Floor” is the main level, “3rd Floor” is the upper level, and “1st Floor” is the basement. Use their shapes and room relationships as layout evidence. Scale calibration, level registration, wall thicknesses, elevations and roof geometry are modeling inferences; list assumptions and report resulting concept areas separately. No survey or dimensioned appraisal sketch has been supplied.

The listing's structured lot size is 1.35 acres while its narrative says 1.33 acres. Preserve this discrepancy; neither value establishes modeled boundaries, setbacks or exact site geometry.

## Program quality and deliverables

Apply the repository's premium defaults as **proposed** choices: a complete kitchen equipment set, wide panel-ready refrigeration, usable clothes storage in every bedroom, primary double vanity, separate shower and tub, enclosed toilet room, coordinated hardware, and recessed/task lighting with selected focal fixtures. Check those choices against the source layout before changing walls. Unconfirmed existing features must not be described as observed facts.

The intended deliverables are an editable native concept, coordinated floor plans for all three occupied levels, and reviewed render coverage of arrival, rear/site, useful interiors and the defining outdoor features. Models, renders and checks are pending at this brief stage. [Program decisions](program.md) distinguish listing evidence, selected concept intent and open work. This is not an as-built record, product specification, engineering design or permit set.
