# Option B — private garden suites and dressing buffer

A proposed 2D option for critique, not a built or verified home. Both king bedrooms sit **behind** the street-facing dressing/bath zone, with headboards at the solid outer walls. The private bath door and cased dressing opening branch separately from each bedroom; neither suite is a through-route. This is a different arrival/privacy sequence from front-bedroom Option A.

**Exact area:** outer planning rectangle 62 × (3,312 ÷ 62) ft = **3,312 sq ft**. Open courtyard 16 × 32 ft = **512 sq ft**. Enclosed planning area = **2,800 sq ft**, including wall allowances and excluding the courtyard. Overall depth is about 53 ft 5.03 in. These are planning-grid areas, not site or cladding-face measurements.

The narrower, longer courtyard gives 19-ft room zones beside 4-ft enclosed galleries. Each suite has a 19 × 13-ft sleeping zone, 7 × 13-ft dressing room and 12 × 13-ft bath planning zone. Mixed dressing contains long hanging, double hanging, drawers, shoes, hamper, mirror and task light. Proposed WC clear reservations are **39 × 66 in**, with 30-in pocket entries. Ensuite doors swing outward into free bedroom space rather than into a basin user; actual door/standing checks remain required.

The service side contains a **12 × 12-ft butler’s pantry**, a useful **24 × 54-in island**, a shared-wall prep counter/sink and substantial shallow east/south shelving. Proposed work/shelf aisles are 42/36 in, with 36-in island-end passages. The island has open lower shelves rather than projecting drawers; counter drawer travel is limited to 18 in to retain 24 in for the user. The 7 × 10-ft laundry holds **two compact stacked washer/dryer pairs**, stain sink, shallow supply storage and dedicated utility storage. Both sinks share the x = 50-ft wet-wall concept. The northern 2-ft strip becomes integrated public-facing full-height bookcases. Equipment/operator access is proposed, not product approved.

**Tradeoffs:** front clerestories now illuminate private dressing/bathing rather than the king bedrooms; the facade must be reconciled if selected. The public rear band is roughly 15 ft 5 in deep, and the courtyard is only 16 ft wide. The pantry island is intentionally modest, and the compact dressing drawer tower needs a real compatible 12-in-travel drawer asset. A dedicated **4 × 6-ft mechanical room** takes 24 sq ft from the living-room corner, leaving about 331 sq ft of living area. Separate air-handler and hot-water planning footprints face 5.5-ft removable panels and a 4-ft gallery service/removal approach. Maintenance temporarily occupies that approach; the opposite courtyard gallery remains available. Exact equipment, ventilation/air volume, acoustics and manufacturer service instructions remain unselected. Those issues are explicit checks before any modeling.

The office/guest room, third shower bath, full kitchen, two complete primary suites and private entry remain. Furniture/fixture/door coordinates and proposed approach rectangles are included in `option-b.json`; they are not certified clearances.


**Bathing and privacy refinement:** both 72-in soaking tubs are inset into deliberately fitted surrounds against the internal suite wall, closing the previous small rear/side gaps. Wall-mounted fillers serve the tub without a floor stem in the approach; maintenance access faces the dry central aisle. The shower has a separate proposed dry entry zone. Front bath/WC/dressing daylight is high and obscured, with an **opaque night shade** required; obscured glass alone does not guarantee freedom from silhouettes. No clear eye-level street view into bathing or dressing is proposed. Compact WCs favor a premium integrated-bidet/TOTO-style concept, with exact product, power/water and service requirements unselected.

**Integrated refrigeration:** the kitchen now reserves paired 24-in cabinet-depth integrated columns (48 in total), a **25-in-deep fitted enclosure**, flush panel fronts and a full-depth aligned overhead cabinet. The return-wall end has a **10.5-in filler**, with **9 in** before the adjoining cooking run. The nominal front aisle to the island is **56 in**. This is a correct product-family allowance, not adoption of the old deeper native fridge asset; selected hinges, handles, ventilation, opening angle and drawer/bin removal still need product-specific checking.

**Laundry operation refinement:** the two stack reservations are now **24 in wide × 33 in deep each**, with a 48-in-deep operating zone across their full fronts. The east stack ends 3 in before the sink-base footprint, removing the former blocked machine front. This requires compact-capacity machines, a deliberate tradeoff of B. Machine and sink occupied zones overlap, so they are intended for sequential use. The entry is a 3-ft pocket retracting south; utility storage fixes to the south wall and stays clear of the pocket cavity. Final products, kits, upper reach and open-door service geometry remain to be checked.

The dressing rooms use 3-ft cased bedroom openings to avoid leaves swinging into bedside circulation; the ensuite remains separately closable. Laundry bodies are now shown at the representative **596 × 643 mm** dimensions (WXR960/TXR960), separately from their 24 × 33 in installed reservations; the 434 mm door projection starts at the actual front plane. Source links and the unapproved 75 mm rear-service planning allowance are recorded in the JSON.
