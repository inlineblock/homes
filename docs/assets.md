# Reusable assets

An asset is something reusable across homes: a tile and its real-world dimensions, a material, a window, a cabinet module, a stool, a pendant light, a plant, or an entire assembly.

Use `library/<category>/<asset-name>/v001/`. Each version contains `asset.json`, editable source or a reproducible generator, and a preview when available. Texture sets belong alongside their material rather than in a global pile of images.

Record a stable ID, version, dimensions in meters, source/author, license or rights status, files, and any dependencies. Materials should record physical scale; texture maps should be identified as base color, roughness, normal, displacement, and so on. Models use meters and Z-up, with a sensible placement origin. Do not embed absolute paths to someone's Downloads folder.

Prefer linking reusable Blender collections and materials from their library file. Store those links as relative paths. Record the exact library version in the home's `project.json`. Do not edit a version that has been adopted by a home: make `v002`, review it, and explicitly update homes that should receive the change. Git history provides a second layer of traceability.

The first original asset is `materials/sage-fluted-tile/v001`: a 3 × 12 inch glazed, vertically ribbed ceramic tile. Its source geometry and material are generated by the shared tools and linked into Atrium 01. This is an original concept material, not a claim of a purchasable manufacturer's product.

Timber Courtyard 02 reuses that tile, the `fixtures/opal-globe-pendant/v001` and `furniture/walnut-counter-stool/v001` collections promoted from Atrium 01, and adds `materials/warm-vertical-cedar/v001` and `materials/charcoal-standing-seam/v001`. The new shaders accompany real board and seam geometry in the home's generator. The shared planting helpers create original geometry without external texture downloads.

For purchased or downloaded assets, record the original URL and actual redistribution terms. Where sharing the source is not allowed, store acquisition instructions and a placeholder rather than the restricted file.

Garage Loft 03 pins its own revised cedar and eight-foot pool table at `v002`, with finer grain and a darker playing surface, while the other homes retain their original versions. Its double parking carriage uses `v002` for repositioned wheel stops. The detailed generic coupes are original project geometry generated by `tools/garage03/cars.py`; they do not use third-party car models or branding.
