# Twin Gables Courtyard brief

Create a new home, keeping Timber Courtyard unchanged. The user accepted the generated front and backyard directions, requested a more uniform side elevation, and authorized native modeling.

Confirmed priorities:
- Eichler-inspired double-gable architecture, not a literal alpine A-frame.
- A private front with opaque entry and screened lower glazing, warm wood appearance, exposed wood-look covered steel framing and a planted courtyard.
- High vaulted public spaces; flat finished ceilings 10–12 ft depending on room.
- Two primary bedrooms, both with their own ensuite and walk-in closet. Each ensuite also has linen storage and a separate toilet room.
- Separate office. Preserve the ability to host guests if comfortably possible; no fixed third-bedroom requirement.
- Overall size is up to the designer. Function and proportions govern.

Premium defaults carried forward: both primary baths receive double vanities, separate tub and shower; kitchen includes cooking, extraction, wide paneled refrigeration, sink, dishwasher, cabinetry and fitted food storage. Include laundry, general storage and service reservations.

Assumptions: one occupied level, illustrative wooded flat site, two passenger-car parking spaces with a separate pedestrian arrival. No actual location, survey, climate classification, product selection or engineering is established. Concept documentation, not permit/construction drawings.
