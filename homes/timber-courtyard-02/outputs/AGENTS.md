# Current home outputs

- Follow the required output organization in `homes/AGENTS.md`: `images/` holds selected renders; `plans/` holds plans, PDFs, presentation sheets, and their previews.
- `README.md` is a visual index with labeled images and links to every current deliverable. Update it when adding, removing, or renaming a deliverable.
- Overwrite the same filenames after review. Keep no dated/versioned output folders or duplicate copies; Git records history. View numbers are not revision numbers.
- Keep drafts in ignored `work/`; inspect actual images and rendered PDF pages before promoting replacements.
- Changes to a source render or plan require refreshing any presentation board or preview that includes it.
- Walkthroughs belong in `videos/`, with stable MP4 filenames, an index, camera-route/verification records and paired native/AI reference frames under `videos/references/`. Keep frame sequences, control maps, failed AI attempts and caches in ignored `work/`; keep model weights outside the repository.
- A walkthrough must show camera travel through the model with real parallax and traversable openings. Do not substitute a slideshow or digital pans/zooms. Preserve the canonical model and document any presentation-only opening states in a separate editable scene. Review both motion and individual frames. Label native animation separately from AI finishing; photographic stills or an unreviewed AI test do not establish a completed AI video.
