# Shared kitchen appliances

- Search this catalog before making a stove, cooktop, oven, dishwasher or hood inside a home. Use pinned linked assets for the appliance, and keep its cabinet opening and service routing in the home.
- A cooking area must identify both cooking surface and oven. An induction rectangle is not a complete oven. Every kitchen must also identify refrigeration, sink, dishwasher, food preparation, storage and an extraction route, unless the brief explicitly excludes an item.
- Remove opaque cabinet carcasses and drawer fronts from appliance openings. Visually inspect the appliance in the finished kitchen, and check door operation and the route past open dishwasher/oven doors.
- Preserve the documented size and front direction; do not stretch an appliance to fill a gap. Handles project beyond nominal cabinetry depth. Use the measured bounds in the manifest when checking collisions.
- These are original generic visualization envelopes, not manufacturer installation drawings. Actual appliance selection, cutouts, service clearances, heat separation and ventilation requirements remain to be coordinated with the selected product and project.
- The hood collection includes a canopy, filters and chimney cover. The host home must provide a plausible continuous extraction route; a cover floating under a solid ceiling is incomplete.
