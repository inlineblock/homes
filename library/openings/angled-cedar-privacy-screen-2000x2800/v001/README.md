# Fixed angled cedar privacy screen

Original **2.0 × 0.30 × 2.8 m** reusable fixed facade screen, with 23 solid cedar fins captured by a slim bronze-gray frame. Width lies along X, front faces -Y, and the origin is bottom-center of the frame at Z=0. Preserve scale and rotate/place the entire linked collection.

![Native screen preview](preview.png)

Each vertical fin is 120 × 25 mm and turns 45° in plan. At 84.09 mm pitch, their normal-view projections overlap by 18.44 mm. Oblique light and views can pass between them; this is geometric screening, not a certified privacy or solar-control product. Cedar uses the pinned shared v003 material; metal uses bronze-gray standing seam v001 as a finish only.

The host supplies fixed top/bottom supports, engineered anchors, drainage, isolation and safe maintenance access. Keep at least 300 mm from the back of the frame to glazing as a concept service gap. This is not a walkable passage; use accessible glazing and demountable frame fixings or another designed maintenance method. Check every operable window's sweep separately.
