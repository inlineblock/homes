These original ImageGen concepts were accepted by the user for architectural direction before modeling. They are not native renders or photographs of an existing home. The new model must coordinate their private street facade, paired Eichler-style gables, tall vaults and open garden elevation; exact dimensions and unseen layout were not established by the images.

The generated side view was rejected for awkward, nonuniform proportions. Use consistent eaves, structural rhythm and window heads in the new side elevation. Do not copy the rejected roof junctions.

Later user requirements govern: two full primary suites, each with ensuite, walk-in closet, bathroom linen storage and enclosed toilet room, plus a separate office. Area is flexible. See [brief](../brief.md), [design intent](../design-intent.md) and [source identities](provenance.json).
