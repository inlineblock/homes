# Coastal House

- Preserve the user's priorities: bright, refined modern coastal architecture, large windows, a long retractable glass wall, and a separate sliding serving window with usable countertops and cabinetry on both sides.
- Rendering quality must come from the editable scene: subtle physical-scale materials, detailed joinery, rounded upholstery, natural daylight and deliberate composition. Increasing resolution alone is insufficient. Do not replace model evidence with an unrelated generated illustration.
- Current concept assumptions are 2,720 gross enclosed sq ft, one story, three bedrooms and two baths. These are design choices, not dimensions supplied by the user. Terrace and eaves are excluded.
- The 30-foot great-room opening and 12-foot counter opening are separate systems. Preserve actual side-pocket cavities, track continuity and open/closed states; never hide the glazing to simulate opening.
- Follow `../AGENTS.md` for current outputs and galleries. Keep only reviewed images in `outputs/images/`, drawings/PDFs in `outputs/plans/`, and drafts in ignored `outputs/work/`. Git stores prior revisions.
- This is a concept on an illustrative coastal backdrop. Site orientation, flood exposure, structure, weather seals, corrosion specification, screens and exact commercial opening systems remain unverified.
