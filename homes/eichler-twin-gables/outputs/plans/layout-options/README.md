# Twin Gables Courtyard — layout alternatives

Three furnished **2D proposals awaiting selection**. Each provides two primary suites, an office/guest room, three full bathrooms, a complete kitchen, a butler pantry, two stacked washer/dryer pairs and an open courtyard. These drawings are not derived from a rebuilt native model and do not replace the current published floor plan.

The target is **2,800 sq ft enclosed on the planning grid, including wall allowances and excluding the open courtyard**. Overall dimensions are concept dimensions; the proposals are not construction documents or product-specific clearance approvals.

## Sources and review

The adjacent `option-*.json` files contain the proposed coordinates; `option-*-rationale.md` records each author's choices and unresolved details. `comparison-source.json` links drawings to exact source hashes and states the review scope. SVG copies are editable presentation drawings.

These are distinct alternatives, not a chronological archive. Once a layout is chosen, coordinate and review that design before rebuilding the home or deriving new photographs. Refrigerator joinery, door hardware, bidet fixture selection, services and engineering still require product-specific coordination.
