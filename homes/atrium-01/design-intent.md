# Atrium 01 — a sheltered courtyard ring

## Brief and evidence

Retain an original Eichler-inspired, single-level home around 2,400 gross enclosed square feet, three bedrooms and three full bathrooms. The kitchen remains the focus: warm walnut, pale stone, sage fluted ceramic and restrained dark metal. Preserve an open-air atrium and expressed post-and-beam roof. The existing plan and actual native views were inspected before this revision: entry required crossing outdoors; storage was incomplete; cabinets backed onto full-height glass; the nominal nine-foot ceiling concealed an approximately 8 ft 4 in beam clearance; the roof lacked a convincing support/weather edge.

This is an illustrative level site with a south approach, not a surveyed parcel or an identified climate. Household assumption: two primary occupants, two flexible bedrooms and six-person everyday dining. No budget, product approval or structural capacity is inferred.

## Directions compared

1. **Repair the existing ring:** move the court slightly north and add an indoor cross-gallery. Efficient area retention, but retaining every old partition would preserve disproportionate secondary rooms and scattered services.
2. **Two parallel pavilions:** separate public and private wings with a glazed link. Legible organization, but more roof junctions/perimeter and circulation expense within the same area.
3. **Raised public roof/clerestory:** stronger spatial hierarchy and potential daylight. Adds height and roof complexity without evidence that the open court cannot supply the needed daylight.

Select the first direction with a complete internal replan, informed by independent program, architecture and site/asset reviews. One disciplined low roof and a clear sheltered route suit this brief better than added massing.

## Selected plan before modeling

Retain the 60 × 44 ft outer rectangle and 16 × 15 ft court: 2,640 minus 240 equals **2,400 sq ft gross enclosed**, including walls; excludes court, carport, canopies and garden terraces. Move the court to x25–41, y13–28 ft. Coordinates below establish zones; finished clear dimensions account for walls and actual fixtures.

- Private west wing: two 13 × 14 ft sleeping zones with adjoining storage; bedroom 2 has a 7 × 8 ft ensuite. A separate 7 × 8 ft laundry serves the bedroom wing. A five-foot nominal gallery connects both rooms directly indoors.
- North-west primary suite: 14 × 16 ft sleeping zone; generous separate bathing zone, double vanity, tub, shower and enclosed WC; a private vestibule separates it from shared circulation. Provide actual wardrobes without narrowing the route beside the bed.
- South entrance: an eight-foot-wide foyer, coat/drop storage and five-foot nominal east–west gallery south of the atrium. Guest/bedroom-3 full bath sits beside this route. Entry connects to both wings without crossing the court.
- Public east wing: a 19 × 20 ft kitchen, a 16 × 16 ft dining room north of the court and a 19 × 16 ft living room. Food pantry and utility/bulk storage occupy the south service strip. Kitchen glazing above counters has an appropriate sill.
- Separate two-car carport and sheltered walk connect parking to a generous front landing. Parking/turning and pedestrian envelopes remain visible in the site drawing, outside conditioned area.

## Character and heights

Use a continuous **10 ft 6 in finished ceiling plane**, **9 ft 6 in clear beneath principal exposed beams**, and a deliberate **9 ft clear service ceiling** where ducts/lighting require it. One occupied floor at datum zero; there is no floor-to-floor height. Record roof structure/insulation depth separately. Carry the exposed timber rhythm into two-foot eaves, a deeper entry canopy and a supported rear sheltered edge. Align posts and mullions rather than implying unsupported sixty-foot beams. Member sizing, lateral design and foundations remain engineering tasks.

Keep warm mineral plaster secondary to timber, sage tile and pale stone; avoid stark white box edges. Kitchen and selected built-in storage use newly published walnut derivatives of existing cabinet families, preserving immutable original pins. Reuse existing full-size beds, chairs, fixtures, lighting, pavers and landscape collections. The courtyard receives an appropriately sized olive-like specimen, a planted bed, a continuous paved edge, real operable glazing and drainage reservations. Large spreading trees belong beyond the roof, with layered native planting and a clear approach.

Inspect arrival, three exterior angles, kitchen in both directions, living, primary bedroom/bath, courtyard and roof-off layout. Independent final visual review precedes promotion. Then generate a full photographic tour from the reviewed native views, preserving architecture while enriching plausible surrounding landscape.
