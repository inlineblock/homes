These do not obscure the principal architectural subjects. Photographic finishing may enrich appearance but must remain labeled and cannot substitute for the native geometry evidence.

The review does not certify structure, stair/guard regulations, pool barriers, waterproofing, exact product installation, energy performance, surveyed site conditions or permit compliance. Plans use project-authored dimensions and an explicit area convention.
