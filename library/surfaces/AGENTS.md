# Reusable paving and tile modules

- Repeated pavers, decking sections and tile units belong here. A shared material alone does not make repeated hand-built paving geometry a shared asset.
- Publish actual module dimensions, thickness, bevel and placement datum. Place with a deliberate joint pitch. Do not stretch a four-foot module to an arbitrary size; use a different module or an explicitly identified project-specific edge cut.
- Use pinned linked collections and record both the surface asset and its material dependencies. Preserve relative paths when publishing.
- `honed-limestone-paver-4ft/v001` is 4 x 4 feet x 1.2 inches with its top at Z=0. A 4.02-foot pitch creates 0.24-inch joints. This is original illustrative geometry, not an outdoor slip rating or commercial product approval.
