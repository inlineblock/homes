## Terrace / open

![Terrace open](images/01-terrace-open.png)

Viewed from inside toward the terrace, the 30-foot glass wall retracts into its left pocket. The separate kitchen window opens over indoor/outdoor cabinets and counters. The revised design places dining beneath a 32 ft wide house-attached timber pergola projecting 15.15 ft, with a ledger above the opening and only two outer posts. Conversation seating at the west end faces inward around its table. Open slats provide shade, not rain protection; structural attachment remains unengineered.

## Roof and site overview

![Roof and parking](images/07-roof-and-parking.png)

A raised 3:12 pavilion steps above lower 1:12 bedroom/entry and kitchen wings. The front wing falls south and the kitchen east; the carport retains its 1:12 slope. Bronze-gray metal, driftwood eaves, mushroom plaster and 4 x 2 ft limestone panels form the coordinated exterior palette. Drainage and roof assemblies remain unengineered concepts.
