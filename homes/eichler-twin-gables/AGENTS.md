# Twin Gables Courtyard

- This is a separate home derived from Timber Courtyard's courtyard idea; do not edit the original home when working here.
- Read brief.md, design-intent.md and program.md. Preserve the approved front and rear reference character; the side concept was explicitly rejected and must be redesigned with a consistent bay/window-head/eave rhythm.
- Two broad parallel Eichler double-gable roofs over full-height vertical walls; never turn them into steep roof-to-ground A-frame cabins. Keep one straight ridge per wing and a lower connector around an open-air courtyard.
- Two equally complete primary suites: each needs a walk-in closet and ensuite with double vanity, separate tub and shower, an enclosed toilet room and linen closet INSIDE the bathroom. Office remains separate; do not count a labeled office as an equipped third bedroom.
- Size may grow for function; use the shared dimensional source for all models/plans/area records. Do not silently treat initial square footage as mandatory.
- Flat finished ceilings are 10–12 ft by room. Public vaults are taller. Measure clear height after beams/covers/services; record floor-to-roof and clear-height figures separately.
- Exposed structure is concept steel with nonstructural wood-look covers. Show steel/cover distinction in records; beam dimensions, connections, loads, fire protection and thermal detailing are not engineered.
- Front entry and street-facing rooms must retain visual privacy. Garden-facing public rooms can open generously to the rear terrace. Show access, hardware and weather protection consistently.
- Link shared library assets and record exact pins in project.json and assets/README.md. No local copies of generic plants, furniture or equipment.
- Follow parent gallery standard: model-derived front/rear/side/site/interiors/plan plus courtyard and suite features, then reviewed ImageGen interpretations. Approved concept references are not substitutes for model-derived gallery images.
