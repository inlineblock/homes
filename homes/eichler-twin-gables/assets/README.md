## Installation interfaces and review limits

- **Covered steel:** the new 1 m stock U-cover is 300 × 400 mm externally, with real 20 mm faces and an open cavity. Only local X length scaling is permitted; section width/thickness stay fixed. Host-specific concept steel is separate. A 10 mm geometric clearance is assumed, not an engineered steel selection. Longitudinal grain elongates under permitted scaling; cross-grain widths remain fixed. Review actual cover joints, supports and clear ceiling heights in the host.
- **Front privacy:** the two fixed 2.0 × 2.8 m screens have angled fins with measured frontal overlap. Oblique privacy is not guaranteed. Reserve the manifest's 0.30 m glazing service gap and check operable glazing, maintenance and street sightlines separately.
- **Kitchen:** five deep drawer bases, separate 36-inch cooktop/30-inch oven/36-inch hood, a 48-inch panel-ready refrigerator, 24-inch dishwasher, sink base, waste pullout and eight pantry shelf modules are actually installed. Preserve real countertop cutouts, hood route, fridge hinge relief, appliance doors, operator standing space and circulation. Closed placement alone is not an operating-envelope pass.
- **Two primary suites:** each has a king bed, modeled walk-in clothes storage, two basin/mirror fixtures, separate 72-inch tub and shower, an enclosed toilet and one closed linen cupboard **inside its own bathroom**. The linen asset retains two actual doors but replaces the wardrobe rail with five shelves. Reserve a conservative 340 mm door projection plus 600 mm operator space; verify host clearance rather than relying on the isolated open preview.
- **Lighting:** recessed fixtures require ceiling cutouts and above-ceiling housing space; the public pendant suspension must meet the actual vault/ceiling. Fixtures represent visual concepts, not rated photometric or installation specifications.
- **Landscape:** modeled specimen bounds do not establish mature spread, climate suitability or root zones. The frozen model corrects the courtyard maple to scale 0.9 and removes the undersized olive. Named maple/oak specimens now follow their 0.9–1.1 manifest ranges; perennial clumps stay within 0.8–1.4. The 115 generic grass/sage instances deliberately retain host visual variation (mixed clumps 0.8–1.25; courtyard grass 0.75). This follows the shared helper’s natural plant variation guidance, but these legacy manifests do not provide numeric scale ranges: this is an explicit host allowance, not a species/maturity or manifest-compliance claim. Plant origins, canopy clearances and seasonal suitability remain separate design reviews.
- **Furniture/environment:** indoor oak furniture used on the illustrative terrace remains a visual selection; a weather-suitable outdoor product or reviewed derivative is needed for specification. All furniture must sit on actual support surfaces, with doors/chairs and circulation checked independently.
