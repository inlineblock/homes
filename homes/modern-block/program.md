# Modern Block — program and decisions

## Basis and evidence

Modern Block is a reference-led architectural concept based on user-supplied visual references, with creative infill expressly authorized for unseen rooms, missing angles and undocumented details. It is an editable design study, **not an as-built survey, verified original floor plan, or construction document**. Reference identities and identifying URLs are deliberately excluded from repository records at the user's request.

The visible design language guides the concept: family courtyard, covered lanai joining a main house and guest/office retreat, operable timber screens, pale limestone lower walls and warm cedar siding above. Photographs establish visual relationships, not concealed rooms, dimensions, structure, site boundaries, orientation or services. All layout below is selected creative infill.

- **Assumed use:** a family household, occasional overnight guests and home working. Premium profile for coordinated equipment, storage and bathing provisions; this is a design choice, not a claim about real occupants.
- **Selected concept:** two occupied levels in the main house, a single-level guest/office pavilion, courtyard pool and shaded connecting lanai. Two-story main house at the right/east side of the principal composition, lanai and retreat at the left/west side. Cardinal terms are model-coordinate conventions, not surveyed orientation.
- **Room count:** four bedrooms, three full bathrooms and one powder room: primary plus two bedrooms upstairs, guest bedroom and bath in the retreat; shared family bath upstairs and public powder downstairs. Office is a separate work room, not an extra bedroom.
- **Parking:** two cars in the west rear garage plus an illustrative driveway maneuvering apron. No parking lift.
- **Target scale:** 542.36 m² / approximately 5,838 sq ft conditioned gross after excluding the 12.04 m² upper stair void. Current outer envelopes: east main 12 × 20 m ground = 240 m²; east upper 12 × 18.2 m = 218.4 m² before void; west retreat 8 × 12 m = 96 m². These are authoring dimensions, not original measurements. Final model-derived takeoff must supersede the target.
- **Area convention:** gross enclosed conditioned floor area measured to exterior enclosure faces, counted once on each occupied level; exclude upper stair void. Exclude 8 × 8 m / 64 m² garage, pool, 10 × 5 m / 50 m² covered lanai, terraces, 3 × 8 m / 24 m² upper covered balcony, roof overhangs, the open entry porch, projecting facade screens/reveals and open landscape. Do not treat the initial rectangle sum as a verified takeoff.
- **Site:** illustrative corner-lot composition. Actual survey, north, grade, boundaries, easements, utilities, tree locations, climate design loads and drainage outlet remain unverified.

## Spatial organization for authoring

The front door should open from a sheltered, legible landing into a broad daylit foyer with immediate coat/drop storage and a view toward courtyard living. The stair belongs beside this foyer rather than inside a bedroom or kitchen. Put garage entry, mud storage, pantry and kitchen on a short connected service route. Locate the primary suite at the quiet front of the upper level; do not make its bath or dressing area a passage to other rooms.

The ground-floor public rooms face the courtyard: kitchen and eight-seat dining lead into a warm living room, and broad openings lead to the covered lanai. The lanai is both usable outdoor seating and a continuous sheltered route to the guest/office pavilion; neither the pool edge nor operable screens may block that route. Keep the retreat's guest bed, clothes storage and office desk simultaneously usable, with bath access from a small private vestibule.

The upper cedar bar contains the primary suite, two secondary bedrooms, a shared family bathroom, linen storage and a family/flex area around the stair landing. Stack upper wet rooms above ground service/bath areas where feasible. Bedrooms must have exterior daylight openings and independent clothes storage. Upper windows need privacy/shade provisions; photo appearance alone does not establish an escape opening.

## Systems and optional features

- **Comfort:** reserve separate main/upper/retreat conditioning zones, outdoor service pads clear of arrival and quiet courtyard seating, accessible ceiling/service chases, fresh-air intake and separate bath exhaust routes. Humidity control is a design requirement to be resolved for the actual local conditions, not proved by a rendered condenser. Reserve a mechanical/service room of 3.0 × 2.8 m (x19..22/y17.2..20) with a 1.0 m hall door and equipment-removal route; product sizing and clearances pending.
- **Water:** group wet rooms where feasible; reserve heater, accessible shutoffs, condensate and drain provisions. Compare central and retreat hot-water delivery before selecting recirculation. Tub filling and simultaneous demand require sizing. A service closet is a reservation, not proof of adequate heat-pump water-heater air volume.
- **Electrical:** service/panel working zone, two EV conduit/charging positions, ordinary furnished-room outlets, network cabinet and physical lighting controls. Solar/battery are optional future provisions, not modeled installed systems. Load calculation and utility capacity unknown.
- **Fireplace:** selected living-room focal hearth with the shared closed-glass insert. The revised conceptual flue is routed outside the occupied upper rooms within the east masonry pier, rather than through the primary WC. Fuel, listed product, installation clearances, combustion air and actual vent routing remain unselected; the generic insert is not a listed product.
- **Deliberately omitted:** parking lift, sauna, gym, wine room and sports court; these are not necessary for the requested reference-led recreation. A second dishwasher, second oven or secondary laundry is optional only if space and actual household need justify it.
- **Mobility:** ground-floor guest accommodation permits a potential entry-level sleeping arrangement if the illustrative approach remains step free. The primary and other family bedrooms depend on stairs; the sheltered guest route crosses the lanai. No lift or accessibility certification is claimed.

## Taller rooms and occupied upper projection

The user selected 12 ft (3.6576 m) clear ceilings downstairs and 10 ft (3.048 m) upstairs, with the cedar upper volume extending 1.2 m (approximately 3 ft 11 in) forward beyond the ground wall. The upper occupied footprint is x10–22 / y−1.2–17. The added strip enlarges the primary bedroom to 57.4 m² gross and primary bath to 31.0 m² gross before wall deductions. No other room or circulation area is reduced. The bath tub, shower and vanity remain behind y0, above the supported ground footprint. The added strip is dry space; this arrangement reduces concentrated loads at the cantilever tip without establishing structural adequacy.

- **Vertical datums:** ground finished floor 0; lowest regular ground ceiling finish +3.6576 m; upper finished floor +4.1148 m (13 ft 6 in); upper ceiling underside +7.1628 m. The 0.4572 m (18 in) floor/service zone is a dimensional reservation between the ground clear ceiling and upper finish, not a selected structural assembly. Kitchen slat bottoms and other fixed ceiling finishes must respect the stated clear height. Focal light fittings may suspend below the ceiling.
- **Stair:** retain x19–21.8 / y8.2–12.5. Two 12-riser flights at 171.45 mm use 11 tread goings each at 280 mm; the half landing and upper floor provide the other rise transitions. Half landing is +2.0574 m. The uppermost separate tread is +3.94335 m, one normal rise below the upper floor. Twenty-two tread objects therefore represent 24 rises. Stair headroom, support, fall protection and professional compliance require separate verification.
- **Area:** main ground 240 m² + guest 96 m² + upper 218.4 m² − stair opening 12.04 m² = 542.36 m² conditioned gross, approximately 5,838 sq ft. With the excluded-from-conditioned 64 m² garage, total enclosed gross is 606.36 m² / approximately 6,527 sq ft. Open lanai, porch, balcony, pool, terraces and facade projections remain excluded; the enclosed occupied cantilever is included.
- **Envelope:** the upper street wall moves to y−1.2, front reveal reaches y−1.65 and privacy-screen outside face reaches y−1.95. The bedroom/bath divider extends to the new front wall, while its doors stay at their previous absolute positions. Balcony-door location remains y12.2–13.4.
- **Structural and service review:** the continuous occupied cantilever, enlarged front glazing, elevated floor datum and taller envelope require engineered framing, deflection/vibration checks, lateral-load continuity, service coordination and revised flashing/thermal details. The 18 in reserve does not prove those systems fit. The model must be checked for actual clear height rather than relabeling a shorter space.
