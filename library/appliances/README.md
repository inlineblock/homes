# Cooking and kitchen appliance gallery

Choose a complete cooking arrangement, then check its fit in the home. These are original, editable **architectural concept assets**, not selected commercial products or engineered installation drawings. Every image below is a native render of the linked asset. The manifest beside each image records the exact version, placement, dependencies and installation assumptions.

| Arrangement | Choose it when | Include with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Range**: cooking surface and oven in one floor-standing appliance | A compact cooking wall or a deliberate range focal point fits the plan | Compatible hood, exhaust route, landing counters, rear services and open-door space |
| **Cooktop + separate oven** | The plan benefits from storage below the cooktop or a more accessible oven position | Both assets, supported oven housing, actual counter cutout, cooling/service space and hood |
| **Cooktop + double wall oven** | The household needs two full oven cavities | Tall cabinet, comfortable reach to both cavities and nearby landing space; two ovens are optional, not a universal premium requirement |
| **Cooktop + oven/speed-oven combination** | The household wants a full oven and a compact second cooking/reheating cavity | Tall housing, safe upper-cavity reach and actual selected-product services; a speed oven does not replace the cooking surface |

## Select, install and contribute

1. Start with the [home kitchen program](../../docs/home-program-checklist.md), inventory this gallery and choose **range OR cooktop plus oven**. Also identify hood/extraction, refrigeration, sink, dishwasher, prep space, everyday storage, pantry storage and waste/recycling.
2. Read the exact manifest and linked review. Preserve scale and front direction. Nominal size, measured body, decorative face, mounting cutout, operating sweep and service allowance are different quantities.
3. Link the native collection and exact version; create real openings in the host countertop and cabinet. Model the hood route, supports, connections and accessible service reserves at concept level. Record unresolved commercial-product decisions explicitly.
4. Review the installed kitchen at human height and in plan, including open doors/drawers and someone using them. An isolated preview is not host clearance evidence. Keep cooking equipment visible and physically usable in the model before final rendering or AI finishing.
5. If a suitable option is missing, preserve the source version, publish a reviewed sibling or new version with derivation, measured interfaces and actual previews, then explicitly adopt it. Do not shrink a standard appliance or make a private per-home copy. Follow the [asset contribution workflow](../../docs/assets.md).
