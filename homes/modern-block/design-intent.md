# Modern Block — design intent before geometry

## Evidence and fixed direction
The user selected a visual direction and granted creative liberty for missing data, plans and angles. Observed features include a courtyard, covered lanai linking a main home to a guest/work pavilion, pivoting timber louvers, limestone base and cedar upper siding. The exterior has tall ground-floor glazing, restrained dark horizontal bands and an offset upper rectangular volume. The visual references establish appearance; all dimensions and hidden room arrangements are independent design interpretations. Identifying source research stays outside this repository at the user's request.

## Alternatives and critique
1. Exterior shell only: quickest visual match, but rejects the requested whole-home workflow and leaves storage, kitchen, stairs and parking unresolved.
2. Small generic courtyard plan: preserves the silhouette superficially but compresses the premium program and undermines the generous pavilion proportions.
3. Courtyard pavilion composition with a coordinated original interior: selected. Preserve the court/lanai/main-block relationships, a complete usable program and two occupied levels; use a dimensional source shared by the native model and drawings. Develop the room layout independently within the selected massing.

## Selected material and spatial moves
Warm limestone continues into the living hearth and sheltered arrival. Use physically scaled shared limestone panels, neutral cedar with real board joints, thin warm dark frames, pale stone floors and oak cabinetry. Avoid oversaturated orange timber and stark black/white striping. The front garden contains a broad-canopy tree and low drought-inspired planting; botanical and irrigation suitability remain unverified. The lanai's movable louvers must be represented in open and closed states from the same camera. The courtyard pool is narrower than the existing 6×12m library option, requiring a reviewed dimensional sibling. Flat-looking roofs have actual positive drainage behind their thin fascia; no roof is an occupied terrace.

## Asset reuse and contribution
Adopt pinned library materials, limestone panels/paving, complete kitchen equipment, bedroom wardrobes, beds, furniture, sanitary fixtures, lighting and hardware. Contribute original shared broad-canopy tree, narrow courtyard pool and pivot louver assembly because the existing catalog lacks compatible forms/sizes. One primary author owns the home Blender binary. Independent agents own reference research, program critique and bounded shared-asset publication.

## Checks and unknowns
Test arrival with two parking spaces, all bedroom storage, actual stair opening/headroom, kitchen appliance operations, primary bath privacy, roof runoff, pool deck routes and louver sweep. Review front, courtyard/rear, elevated roof/site, kitchen, living, primary bath, primary bedroom and both louver states. Render the same model for every view. Complete a generous photographic tour only after native visual review.

Site boundaries/orientation/grade, original room dimensions, structural/MEP engineering, pool barrier approval, glazing specifications and local permits are not established by this work. These professional/source limitations remain visible in the project records.

## Selected façade revision — recorded before modeling
Independent façade, arrival and layout critiques identified repeated full-height front windows, a shallow entrance and unrelated side apertures as the principal weaknesses. Select an outward projecting limestone arrival portal with cedar soffit, a wider recessed-looking bedroom window framed by 450 mm external reveals, and a separate fixed cedar privacy screen across the primary bathroom bay. The solid pier between these unequal upper bays aligns with the actual bedroom/bath partition. Raise the bathroom glazing sill for privacy.

Give the east elevation a full-height limestone fireplace pier with the conceptual flue routed outside the upper toilet room, and align the stair glazing across both floors while retaining the visible floor edge. These changes add useful depth without taking conditioned floor area. Reject an inward entrance recess because it would consume living space and collide with furniture; reject purely decorative random fins and changes that merely disguise the flat façade in photographs.

Use three equal exterior risers, a level covered entrance landing and a supported driveway connection. Add six shared canopy trees and grouped understory around the existing garden, then authorize coherent surrounding woodland in photographic studies. Landscape context is illustrative, not a surveyed site. Add a dedicated side-elevation camera to judge the pier, openings and entrance together. Review new native geometry, plans, privacy clearances and every photographic view before promoting this revision.

## Upper projection and ceiling proportions — selected before revised geometry
Compare a 0.6 m shallow projection, 1.2 m occupied cantilever and 1.8 m deep projection. Select 1.2 m (approximately four feet): it makes the cedar upper volume visibly float forward, shelters arrival and adds usable bedroom/bath perimeter space while preserving the lower pavilion's prominence. Move the occupied front wall, floor, roof and front reveals together; keep the east flue pier and wet fixtures on their established footprints. Absorb the former independent entrance canopy into the continuous timber soffit with a shallow stone portal header.

Choose 12 ft (3.6576 m) clear ceilings in ground-floor occupied rooms and 10 ft (3.048 m) upstairs. Reserve 18 in (0.4572 m) between the lower finished ceiling and upper finished floor for structure/services; upper finished floor is 4.1148 m. These are concept allowances, not a verified cantilever structure. Recess kitchen slat backing so the slats retain the full 12 ft clear height. Retain human-scale furniture and fittings; coordinate taller ground glazing, upper windows, roof edges, pendant mounting and 24 stair risers. Keep the three-metre rigid lanai screens and give the taller opening an intentional fixed glazed transom. Review ceiling heights from human-eye interior cameras as well as exterior proportions. Site, structural and service engineering remain unverified.

Final spatial coordination retains the primary shower over the supported footprint and supplies a 2.3 m-high, 160 mm-thick stone service fin behind its controls. The fin meets the east wall and leaves a usable dry daylight strip at the new front glazing, reached around its west end. It is a plumbing/waterproofing reservation, not a cantilever support. Lower arrival cameras make the occupied projection legible.

## Concealed kitchen and shared component kit — selected before modeling

Adapt the requested kitchen character through cream inset joinery, a natural-oak paneled island, restrained aged brass, subtly veined cream stone, concealed refrigeration and two drawer-front dishwashers. Original component dimensions respond to this home's room; they are not measurements of an existing kitchen. Publish reusable parts as versioned native collections with closed and open states, real internal cavities, documented mounting and operating allowances. Keep reference media and identity outside the repository.

Compare three arrangements: a finish-only replacement retains an undersized cooking aisle and misses the concealed-equipment intent; a full perimeter duplication would conflict with the courtyard glazing; a window-wall cleanup/cooking arrangement with a freestanding prep island fits the established house. Select the third. Move the sink beneath the existing rear window, flank it with concealed dishwashers, place a cream-and-brass 48-inch range and matching extraction hood to the window's east, and retain 48-inch refrigeration at the northeast end. Give refrigeration a real landing surface, hinge relief, ventilation and a replacement route. Independently check the two dishwasher doors and oven doors against the shortened island.

Use a 3.40 × 1.45 m island with four seats, open prep surface, drawer storage, a microwave drawer and separate waste/recycling pullout. A pantry appliance garage, glazed display storage, matching pulls/knobs, bridge faucet, picture light, shaded sconces and a two-shade pendant extend the coordinated kit. Cabinet and appliance families are reusable; the fitted counters, service routing and exact room arrangement stay home-specific. Keep equipment at rigid physical scale.

Preserve the selected 12-foot clear kitchen height, recessed slat backing, rooflight, courtyard glazing and house envelope. Tall joinery has a deliberate termination below the ceiling; lighting hangs at useful human scale without lowering the room. The kitchen interprets the warmer joinery vocabulary within Modern Block's existing architecture. Cooking extraction, gas/electrical/water connections and ventilation remain concept reservations pending product selection and professional coordination. Review native closed/open views and the furnished plan before updating the photographic studies.
